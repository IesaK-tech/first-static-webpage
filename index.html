Albert Einstein

This page will tell you what Einstein did and what he is famous for

Albert Einstein was born on the 14th March 1879. He was a German man and was heavily known for developing the theory of relativity. His career was in physics, being a theoretical physicist and had studied at the Swiss Federal Polytechnic school in order to get his diploma (1900). He also obtained a PhD from the University of Zurich (1905). He played a crucial role in helping the US government too when he was given an American Citizenship in 1940. On the eve of World War 2, he endorsed a letter to the president alerting him about German nuclear weapons, and recommended that the US should begin similar research, which became the Manhattan Project. Some of the other things he was respectfully known for was:
  • General Relativity
  • Special relativity
  • Photoelectric effect
